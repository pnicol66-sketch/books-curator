Books Curator — Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

Books Curator is a photography app for cataloguing books. It runs entirely in your web browser on your own phone. There is no Books Curator server, no account to create, and no company collecting anything about you.

The short version. Your photographs stay on your phone and in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to us, because there is no "us" to send it to — the app has no backend of any kind.

What the app stores, and where

  • On your phone. Your shelf photographs, the details you type (shelf labels, notes, counts, your name) and your settings are held in your browser's local storage on the device. They are not transmitted anywhere by the app.
  • In your Google Drive, if you choose. When you tap "Upload", the app copies that shelf's photographs and a small text record into a folder in your own Google Drive account. You own those files.

What the app can see in your Google Drive

The app requests a single, narrow Google permission: drive.file. This limits it to files and folders it creates itself, or that you explicitly hand to it. It cannot see, read, or list anything else in your Drive.

Sharing a folder

Some copies of this app are set up to work with a curator's archive. In that case, the first time you upload, the app asks whether to share the Books Curator folder with a named email address, which is shown to you in the prompt.

  • Sharing is read-only — the other person can view and download, not alter or delete.
  • It happens only if you agree. Decline and nothing is shared.
  • It covers that folder alone. Nothing else in your Drive is affected.
  • You remain the owner and can stop sharing at any time from Google Drive itself.

What the app sends over the internet

Only to Google, and only on your instruction:

  • accounts.google.com — to sign you in to your own Google account
  • www.googleapis.com — to upload your photographs to your own Drive

The app uses no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services of any other kind. Voice dictation, if you use it, is provided by your phone's browser and follows its own privacy terms.

Motion sensors

While the camera is open the app reads the phone's tilt sensor to draw a level line in the viewfinder. The readings are used on the spot and never stored or sent.

Deleting your data

Delete any shelf from the home screen, or everything the app holds from Settings → Delete ALL app data on this phone. Photographs already in your Google Drive are yours to keep or delete there.

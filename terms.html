What the app is

The app guides you through photographing shelves and individual books, shows each photograph under a magnifier so you can check it is readable before you keep it, and on your instruction copies the photographs into your own Google Drive. When your curator would like a closer look at a book, the app shows you the request and guides you through photographing the front of its jacket (or its cover) and its copyright page, and, if you like, typing or dictating the copyright page's words. It does not identify, grade or value anything itself. Identification and documentation are done separately, by Books Curator.

A price printed on a book, or added by a bookseller or past owner, may show in its photographs: it is part of the book, not a statement of what the book is worth.
